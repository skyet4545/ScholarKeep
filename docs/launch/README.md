# Launch Runbook — how the pieces fit

_The customer-acquisition sequence, June 12 → July 31, 2026. Every asset referenced below already exists and is paste-ready._

## The funnel, in one line

FB group value posts → DM → TestFlight beta (Lifetime Pro hook) → feedback + testimonials → App Store launch posts + email sequence → founder-price conversions before July 31.

## Week-by-week

### Now (week of June 12) — beta recruiting. Blocker: Carlos, ~30 min.
1. **Get the TestFlight public link**: App Store Connect → ScholarKeep → TestFlight → External Testing group → enable Public Link → copy. _(The single action everything else waits on.)_
2. **Post** [community-launch-posts.md](community-launch-posts.md) **Post 1** (the July 31 checklist value post) in ONE big group where you're a known member — "Florida Homeschoolers" or "Step Up For Students Family."
3. When someone comments/DMs, reply with the paste-ready response in [recruitment-message.md](../internal/recruitment-message.md) ("When a mom DMs you yes") with the TestFlight link filled in.
4. Ramsey posts **Post 5** (peer voice) in HIS groups — different circles, double the surface area.

### Week 2 (June 15-21) — widen + follow up
- **Post 2** (5 reimbursement mistakes) in the program-specific groups (FES-UA Parents, PEP groups).
- **Post 6 reply scripts** whenever deadline/receipt threads appear organically — highest-conversion, lowest-risk touches.
- Day-5 follow-up DM to every installed tester (script in recruitment-message.md).

### Weeks 3-4 (June 22 – July 5) — App Store submission + testimonials
- Finish the App Store submission (~90-min session with me: screenshots, subscription metadata, reviewer notes — punch list already in task #69). Paste-ready listing copy: [app-store-listing.md](app-store-listing.md).
- Day-14 testimonial asks to happy testers; quotes go on the marketing site.
- On approval: **Post 3** (founder announcement) in established groups, swap site CTA to App Store link.

### Weeks 5-7 (July 6-31) — the deadline push
- [launch-email-sequence.md](launch-email-sequence.md) Emails 1-2 on launch, Email 3 ~July 10-15, Email 4 when founder spots run low or ~July 24-28.
- Beta testers get the **bonus note** (bottom of email sequence), never the buy-now emails.
- IG **Posts 7-8** with real app screenshots.
- Update App Store **Promotional Text** to the post-deadline version on Aug 1 (edits without resubmission).

## Channel priority (from the audience research)

| Channel | Trust level | Cost | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| FB groups, personal posts (Carlos + Ramsey) | Highest | Free | Now |
| Reply scripts in others' threads | Highest | Free | Always |
| Co-op / church / WhatsApp warm DMs | Highest | Free | Now |
| Testimonials on marketing site | High | Free | Week 3+ |
| App Store organic ("Florida ESA," "Step Up receipts") | Medium | Free | At launch |
| Podcast sponsor reads (Schoolhouse Rocked, Little by Little) | Medium-high | ~$200-400 | Post-launch, if signal is good |
| FPEA newsletter / next convention | Highest | $$ | 2027 cycle (May 2026 passed) |

## Hard rules (do not break)

- Posts come from **personal accounts in groups where you're established** — never a brand page, never a cold account.
- **Never** "my husband and I" — Carlos and Ramsey are two dads who are friends. Wives do 95% of the receipts; that's the honest hook.
- **Never** claim verdicts are "official" — always "based on the official Purchasing Guide."
- **Never** imply the app is scholarship-reimbursable — lead with the honest "pay with your own card" note.
- Don't cross-promote from the other product lines' pages (Bible Copilot etc.) without an explicit decision — different brands, wrong audiences, and it dilutes both.

## Success metrics

- **Week 1:** 3-5 TestFlight installs from one post
- **Week 4:** 10+ testers, 2-3 testimonial quotes, App Store approved
- **July 31:** 100 founder-price spots claimed = ~$3,000 first-year revenue + the deadline as proof the app earns its keep
