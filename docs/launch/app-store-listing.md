## Description
**Limit: 4000 characters. This draft is ~2,250 — comfortably under.** _(Updated June 12: added iCloud cross-device sync — shipped in v0.7.1 — and corrected founder framing to two dads.)_

```
The Florida ESA reimbursement deadline is July 31. ScholarKeep helps you get there without the shoebox of receipts and the late-night portal panic.

Built by two Florida homeschool dads for their own families (FES-UA, year 3—and yes, our wives do most of the receipts; we built this for them first), ScholarKeep turns scholarship receipt-keeping from a July nightmare into a few quiet minutes a week.

WHAT IT DOES
• Scan a receipt in one tap. It pulls the merchant, date, and total for you.
• Check eligibility on the spot. Type or tap an item and get a clear verdict—eligible, needs pre-authorization, or not covered—with the actual line of the official Purchasing Guide it's based on. You decide; the app just hands you the reference.
• Track every claim from draft to submitted to paid, so you always know where you stand.
• Export a reimbursement-ready PDF when it's time to submit. The part that turns the last week of July from four hours into a few minutes.
• Forward email receipts straight from Mail.
• Sync across your iPhone and iPad automatically.

WORKS FOR YOUR PROGRAMS
FES-UA, FES-EO, and PEP. If you have kids on more than one program, ScholarKeep keeps them straight.

YOUR RECORDS STAY YOURS
No servers we operate. No account to create. No analytics. No selling your family's data. If you use more than one device, your records sync through your own iCloud account—Apple's encryption, your Apple ID, never a company server. You can delete everything anytime, from inside the app.

AI THAT HELPS, YOU DECIDE
The smart parts—reading receipts, suggesting categories—run on your phone, not in a corporate data center. ScholarKeep never categorizes a $400 curriculum bundle behind your back. It hands you the official reference and lets you make the call.

A FRACTION OF THE COST
Other tools in this space run $144 to $228 a year. ScholarKeep is $39.99/yr (about $3.33/mo) or $4.99/mo. The first 100 Florida families get a founder price of $29.99/yr, locked in for as long as you keep your subscription. There's a free trial, so you can see the whole thing before you decide. A free tier covers eligibility checks, basic receipt tracking, claim lists, and deadline reminders—no artificial caps.

PRO INCLUDES
• Submission-ready PDF export
• CSV export
• Year-end summary
• Family Sharing—one subscription covers your whole household, not per child

ONE HONEST NOTE
ScholarKeep itself is not a scholarship-eligible expense. Parent administrative tools aren't reimbursable—same category as tax software. Pay with your own card. We'd rather tell you that than sell you a workaround.

NOT AFFILIATED
ScholarKeep is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Step Up For Students, AAA Scholarship Foundation, or the Florida Department of Education. Eligibility verdicts are based on the official Purchasing Guides and are meant to help you decide, not to replace official guidance.

Made in Florida, for Florida homeschool families. Get your receipts ready before July 31.
```
