## POST 1 — Value post: the July 31 deadline checklist (soft app mention)
**Where:** Large umbrella groups — "Florida Homeschoolers," "Step Up For Students Family." Best as a standalone helpful post mid-June through mid-July.
**Why it works:** It's genuinely useful on its own. The app mention is one line at the very end and easy to ignore.

> **The July 31 reimbursement deadline is closer than it feels — here's my "don't-lose-money" checklist 🗓️**
>
> Every year I tell myself I'll stay on top of receipts, and every year it's the back half of July with a folder on the kitchen table. If you're submitting for FES-UA, FES-EO, or PEP, here's what I run through before the cliff:
>
> 1. **Match every receipt to a category.** If you can't say which line of the Purchasing Guide an item falls under, the SFO probably can't either.
> 2. **Check for anything that needed pre-authorization** (think tech, certain therapies). A receipt for something that needed a pre-auth you skipped = a denial waiting to happen.
> 3. **Itemized receipts only.** A credit card statement isn't a receipt. You need the line items.
> 4. **Separate what you already submitted** from what's still sitting in the pile, so you're not double-submitting or missing one.
> 5. **Give yourself a buffer.** The portal gets slow the last week of July. Don't be uploading at 11:58pm on the 31st (ask me how I know 🙃).
>
> What's the one thing that always trips y'all up at deadline time? Drop it below — happy to compare notes.
>
> _(Full transparency: my husband and I built a little iPhone app called ScholarKeep that does this checklist for us — scans the receipts, flags the pre-auth ones, keeps submitted vs. not-submitted straight. Everything stays on your phone, nothing goes to a server. It's free during our beta right now if anyone wants to kick the tires — comment and I'll share details. But honestly the checklist above works on paper too.)_

## POST 2 — Value post: 5 common reimbursement mistakes (soft app mention)
**Where:** FES-UA and PEP program-specific groups, where moms are deep in the weeds and trust practical detail.
**Why it works:** Specific, Florida-correct vocabulary signals "this person actually does this." The app mention is a P.S.

> **5 reimbursement mistakes I've made (so you don't have to) 💸**
>
> Three years on the scholarship and I've made every one of these at least once:
>
> 1. **Buying first, checking eligibility second.** It's so much easier to confirm an item's covered *before* the card swipe than to eat a $300 mistake after.
> 2. **Trusting memory over a record.** "I'm pretty sure I submitted that" is how things fall through the cracks. Write it down the day you buy it.
> 3. **Non-itemized receipts.** A total isn't enough — they want the line items. Ask for the detailed receipt at checkout.
> 4. **Forgetting pre-authorization on the stuff that needs it.** This is the big one for tech and some therapies. No pre-auth, no reimbursement, full stop.
> 5. **Cramming it all into the last week.** Tired-brain July receipt sorting is where the errors live.
>
> The honest fix for most of these is just "don't do it on paper at 10pm." Whatever system you use — even a Notes list and a folder — having it written down the day-of beats reconstructing it in July.
>
> _P.S. We ended up building an iPhone app for our own FES-UA family because the paper version was beating me — it checks eligibility against the actual Purchasing Guide line, tracks claims, and reminds you about pre-auths. It's local-only (nothing leaves your phone) and free during beta. Comment "beta" if you want to try it. No pressure though — the list above is the real value._
